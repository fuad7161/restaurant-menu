How it works

Four steps from sitting down to paying.

1

Scan the table

Every table has its own printed QR code. The menu opens in the phone's browser.

2

Order together

Friends at one table share one cart, each from their own phone.

3

Kitchen cooks

Orders appear on the kitchen tablet with a sound. Guests see a live ready time.

4

Pay and review

Guests ask for the bill and pay cash, bKash, Nagad or card at the table.
