How it works

Four steps from sitting down to paying.

1

Scan the table

Every table has its own printed QR code. The menu opens in the phone's browser.

2

Order together

Friends at one table share one cart, each from their own phone.

3

Kitchen cooks

Orders appear on the kitchen tablet with a sound. Guests see a live ready time.

4

Pay and review

Guests ask for the bill and pay cash, bKash, Nagad or card at the table.

Built for busy restaurants

Everything runs in the browser on the phones and tablets you already have.

Bangla and English

Guests switch language at any time. Staff screens work in both too.

Kitchen screen

New, cooking and ready columns, with tickets that change colour as they wait.

No fake orders

Staff accept each order, and ordering can be limited to your Wi-Fi.

Clear bills

VAT and service charge shown up front, and a receipt guests can keep.

Reports

Sales by day, dish and hour, a closing report and CSV export.

Your brand

Your logo and colour on every screen and every printed QR card.

Ready in ten minutes

Add your menu, print the QR cards and put them on the tables.
