Add a staff member

You set each staff member's password, and you can look it up here. Staff can't change it themselves. Each role sees only its own screens. Kitchen: accepts orders, marks them ready, sold-out list. Waiter: takes orders, marks them served, answers calls, sold-out list. Cashier: counter, takes payment, closing report. Manager: runs this branch like the owner (menu, tables, staff, bills, discounts, reports), but can't change VAT or service charge, other branches, or managers. Only the owner and managers remove items from a bill, close a bill without payment, and give discounts.
